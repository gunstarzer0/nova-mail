Privacy policy

Nova mail · last updated 11 November 2026

Who uses it

Nova mail is a personal tool used only by its owner, Sean Claremont, with his own Google account. It isn't offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it uses one Google permission: gmail.readonly, to read messages in the owner's Gmail. It can't send, delete or change anything in that account.

How the data is used

  • To show the owner his own messages, summarize new mail for him, alert him to mail that needs him, and prepare draft replies for him to approve.
  • The text of a message may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write a summary or a draft reply. Nothing else is shared with anyone, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.
  • Short summaries and drafts are kept on the owner's own computer at home. Full messages stay in Gmail.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Nova mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the support email shown on Google's consent screen for this app.
