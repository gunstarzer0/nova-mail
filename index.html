Nova mail

A personal tool, not a public service.

Nova mail is part of Nova, a home assistant that one person, Sean Claremont, runs on his own computer at home. It lets Nova read Sean's own Gmail with read-only access, so she can tell him what came in and prepare draft replies for him to approve. Replies are sent from Nova's own Gmail account, with Sean copied, and only after he approves each one.

It isn't offered to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it.
